## Challenge

The task was to identify a Panama-flagged cargo vessel, its beneficial owner, and latest Vargstad arrival. `MV NORDLYS TRADER` matches IMO `9678234`. Its port history gives March 9, 2026. The registry separates registered owner Meridian Maritime from beneficial owner Nyrden Holdings.
